Friendly FAQ

Questions parents actually ask.

We updated these from the top 20 questions in our support inbox last month. No fluff, promise.

Is it really ad-free? +
Yes — zero ads, zero cross-promos, zero "buy 500 gems" pop-ups. Your subscription is the only business model. If we ever change that, every parent gets an email before we do.
What ages is Bumble & Sprout for? +
We're tuned for ages 4 to 10. Content auto-adapts to each kid's level — siblings on the same account don't see the same missions.
How much screen time does a day take? +
The daily loop is 18–22 minutes. You can set a hard cap from the parent dashboard, and the app politely ends the session with a fun "see you tomorrow" scene.
Does it work offline? +
Yes. Download a week of missions for long flights, car trips, or patchy wifi. Progress syncs the next time you reconnect.
What does it cost after the trial? +
€9/month or €79/year for up to 4 kid profiles. Teachers get classroom licenses free for the first year — just ask us.
How do I cancel? +
Two taps in the parent dashboard. No phone calls, no "are you sure" guilt trips, no hidden checkboxes. We'd rather you come back later than feel trapped now.
