§03 — MANIFESTO

Design is not content. Design is a CLAIM. We don't decorate your product — we make it pick a fight.

WE BELIEVE

A brand that is "safe" is a brand that is invisible. Invisible brands don't survive recessions, algorithms, or indifference. We make brands that can be described in one sentence at a dinner table.

WE REFUSE

Glassmorphism. Endless retainers. Calls that should have been emails. Decks that live in Notion. "AI-generated hero illustrations." Purple. Soft shadows. Stock founders pointing at laptops. Case studies written in LinkedIn voice.

WE CHARGE

In project rates, not hourly. If we finish fast, you keep the time. We sign one NDA per decade and it's already been used. References available in print, stapled, and mailed.
