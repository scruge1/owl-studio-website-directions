Before You Reserve

Every question worth asking.

When will my pair actually arrive?
The first 500 pairs ship June 18, 2026 from Copenhagen. Reservations 501–3000 ship in fortnightly tranches through December. You will be notified with tracking four days before your pair leaves the atelier.
Is the €40 reservation refundable?
Completely. We hold your serial number for seventy-two hours once we contact you for final payment. If you pass, the €40 returns to your card within three business days and the slot passes to the next person in queue.
What is the warranty?
Five years, parts and labour. Drivers are individually measured before shipment and we keep your measurement file on record for lifetime recalibration — a service unique to NOCTURNE.
Can I audition before committing?
Yes. We have partnered with eleven dealers across Europe, North America and Japan. Enter your postal code during checkout and we will route you to the nearest private demo room.
How does NOCTURNE compare to Focal Utopia or Sennheiser HD 800S?
NOCTURNE is closed-back with active noise cancellation and full wireless, which makes direct comparison to open-back references unfair. Against closed-back flagships (Sony MDR-Z1R, Focal Stellia, DCA Stealth) our measurements sit within a hair of the best, at roughly half the price.
What codecs does it actually support?
LDAC, aptX Lossless, LC3 and Auracast are supported natively. Wired USB-C bypass delivers true 24-bit / 192 kHz with zero Bluetooth involvement — recommended for critical listening.
