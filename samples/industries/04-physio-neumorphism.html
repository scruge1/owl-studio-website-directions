02 · Our approach

Three things we do differently.

Most of what patients tell us they wanted from a physio in the past — time, continuity, honesty — sits in the three blocks below. None of it is clever. It’s just how the practice has always been run.

I

One hour per session.

First appointment is sixty minutes, follow–ups are forty–five. You get the full time with the physiotherapist — no shared treatment rooms, no handing you off to an assistant for the exercise half. One patient, one clinician, one hour.

II

Hands–on plus exercise.

Manual therapy alone wears off. Exercise alone without guidance often re–injures. Every session pairs the two — hands–on work to settle tissue, then a small set of exercises (two or three, not twenty) that you actually do between visits.

III

Discharged when you’re fit.

No block–of–six or block–of–ten packages. You come back for the sessions you need and stop when the issue is resolved. Most people finish in three to five visits. If you need only one, you pay for only one.

06 · Insurance & claims

Approved provider for the four major insurers.

We’re an approved physiotherapy provider for VHI, Laya Healthcare, Irish Life Health and GloHealth — which covers the large majority of Irish private medical policies. Bring your policy number on the first visit; we’ll submit the claim for you.

Uninsured? A Med 1 receipt is issued the day of every appointment. Twenty per cent of your physiotherapy spend comes back through the year–end tax return.
