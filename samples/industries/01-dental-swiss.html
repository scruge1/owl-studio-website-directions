01 · What to expect

Forty minutes, no upsell.

A new–patient exam covers a full soft–tissue check, two bitewing X–rays, a written treatment plan and, if nothing pressing is found, a hygiene appointment booked on the way out. You will leave with a printed plan and a cost for every line item on it.

02 · Fees & insurance

We publish every price.

We accept VHI, Laya Healthcare and Irish Life Health direct claims, and we complete PRSI Treatment Benefit forms at reception. Card payments over the counter. No dental finance, no “smile makeover” packages.

03 · What to bring

Three things, that’s it.

Your PPS number if you want us to claim your PRSI exam. Your insurance membership number if you have one. And the name and dose of any medication you take — we ask because bisphosphonates and anticoagulants change how we treat you.
