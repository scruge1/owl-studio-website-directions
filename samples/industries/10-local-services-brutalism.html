Plain answers

No sales-speak · no hedging
Do you take fridges, freezers, and mattresses?

Yes. Fridges and freezers are €25 each — there’s a compressor-oil handling fee built into the WEEE facility in Raheen. Mattresses are €18 each, no exceptions, single or double. If you’re doing a full-house clearance these are included in the house-clearance price, not extra.

Will you take just one piece of furniture?

Yes. Single-item pickups are €65 inside Limerick city (one man, one van). Two-man pickup if it’s coming from upstairs is €95. If it’s reusable we’ll offer to donate it to SVP on your behalf and issue you the donation docket.

How do I know the waste actually goes somewhere licensed?

You get a tip voucher. Every single job. It shows the facility name, the weight, the date, and the weighbridge ticket number. If we’re on your RTB paperwork, your insurance claim, or a commercial lease handback, the voucher is what you show them.

Our waste permit WCP-2021-00472 is on the public register at Limerick City & County Council. If anyone — a landlord, a solicitor, a buyer’s surveyor — wants to verify us, ring 061 557 200 and ask. That’s why we’re listed.

Do I need to be there while you work?

No. Probably half the jobs we do, the owner isn’t on-site. Leave a key with a neighbour, a solicitor, a letting agent, or in a key-safe. We photograph the job before we start and after we finish, and you get the photos by WhatsApp or email the same evening. Cash payment on the day or bank transfer — your call.

Cash or card? How does payment work?

All of them. Cash, debit, credit, Revolut, bank transfer. We carry a SumUp card reader. You get a VAT-inclusive receipt every time, no exceptions. For commercial and landlord jobs we invoice with 14-day terms if you prefer. The one thing we don’t do is “price agreed on the day, mate” — the quote is written and fixed before we start.
