Section 05 How we work

Three decisions that shape every file.

These are the operating choices clients ask about most often. We publish them here so you know before signing the engagement letter whether our approach fits yours.

§ A · Pricing

Fixed fees where the job allows it.

Company accounts, CT1s, Form 11s and payroll are quoted as fixed annual fees. Audit representation, disputed tax and one–off advisory are hourly. The engagement letter you sign names the deliverables, the fee and the end date. If scope changes, we email before we bill.

Annual · Hourly · Project
§ B · Deadlines

Filed three weeks early, not three weeks late.

Company CT1s are drafted in month seven of the nine–month window. Form 11s are circulated in the first week of October. October 31st is a review date here, not a filing date. If your records reach us by month six we guarantee the early draft; after that we queue in order of arrival.

ROS agent · 9–month CT1 · 31 Oct Form 11
§ C · Software

Cloud accounting — Xero, Sage or Surf.

New clients who are still on spreadsheets are migrated to one of Xero, Sage Business Cloud or Surf Accounts within the first three months. We are Xero Certified Advisors but we do not push it where another package fits better. No commission from any vendor, ever.
