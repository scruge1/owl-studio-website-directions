Three generations

The Callanans — three generations, one doorway on O'Connell Street.

Liam Callanan opened the practice in 1987 after fourteen years dispensing in Dublin. His daughter Aoife took over in 2002, and since 2019 Liam's granddaughter Mairéad has led the clinical work alongside her brother Dónal on the dispensing side. Four decades on the same street, and most of the week is still the same families walking in.

What the state and your insurer
already cover.

Bring your PPSN and VHI/Laya membership number. We submit the claim at reception — no forms at home.

Dept of Social Protection

PRSI Treatment Benefit

If you've paid PRSI for 260+ weeks (or 39+ weeks in the last two years), you qualify for a free or subsidised sight test and a contribution towards standard frames.

Sight test ~€42 covered
Standard frames €42 off
VHI · Laya · Irish Life

Health insurance cover

Most plans include an annual optical allowance. VHI Company Plan, Laya Connect, and Irish Life 4D typically contribute between €75 and €200 towards your frames, plus one free eye test per year.

Frame allowance up to €200
Eye test 1 × free/yr
Revenue

Med 1 tax relief

Eye tests are considered qualifying medical expenses under Revenue's Med 1 scheme. Claim 20% back on anything you pay above what PRSI and insurance cover.
