Worth booking a test

Signs you may need a hearing test.

Most hearing loss happens gradually, and the people around us notice it before we do. If any of the six points below sound familiar, a free 20-minute screening will tell you where you stand.

1

You ask people to repeat themselves often

Especially in busy rooms, on the phone, or when someone is speaking from behind you. If “sorry, what did you say?” has become a habit, it’s worth checking.

2

The TV is too loud for others in the room

Family or a partner regularly asks you to turn the volume down. You can hear the TV clearly, but they find it uncomfortable. That gap is a useful early indicator.

3

Ringing, buzzing, or hissing in the ears

Tinnitus often accompanies hearing loss and is very treatable. If you hear a tone that isn’t coming from outside, we can help you understand why and what to do about it.

4

Missing the start of sentences in meetings

You catch the middle and the end but the first few words slip past. This is a classic pattern of high-frequency loss and is often the first thing patients mention at work.

5

Struggling to follow conversation in restaurants

Background noise makes it hard to separate voices from clatter. If you dread dinners out or sit quietly while others chat, your hearing may be doing more work than it should.

6

Children or grandchildren sound mumbled

Higher-pitched voices go first with age-related hearing loss. If adult voices are fine but small children and women’s voices are harder to follow, that pattern is worth testing.

Your visit

What to expect at your appointment.

We run on time, we explain every step, and we never pressure anyone into buying a hearing aid. Here is what your visit will look like from the moment you walk in.

1

Arrival & paperwork

You’ll be met at reception, offered tea or water, and asked to fill in a one-page medical history. Arrive ten minutes before your slot the first time.

2

Hearing screening

A 20-minute screening with pure-tone audiometry. You listen through headphones and raise your hand when you hear each tone. Painless and straightforward.

Free — no obligation
3

Full assessment if needed

If the screening suggests hearing loss, we book a 45-minute full assessment. This includes speech-in-noise testing, tympanometry, and a plain-English explanation of the audiogram.

€75 — one-time
4

Treatment options — no pressure

We walk through your options: wait-and-watch, lifestyle changes, or a hearing aid if clinically indicated. You take the information home. No same-day sales tactics, ever.

Plain-English pricing.

From €1,800 per ear for entry-level digital aids, up to €3,400 per ear for premium AI-adjusted models. You pay per ear because most patients need two, but single-ear fittings are available if that’s what your hearing test shows.

Most Irish Life Health and VHI policies cover €760 per aid every four years. Bring your policy number and we process the claim for you — no paperwork on your side.

If you are on PRSI Class A, E, H, P or S, the HSE Treatment Benefit Scheme adds another €500 per aid every four years. We check your entitlement at the assessment.

Your audiologist

Dr. Maeve Fitzgerald, PhD.

Maeve qualified with a BSc in Audiology from University College Cork in 2007 and completed her PhD at UCC in 2013, where her thesis focused on paediatric hearing loss in rural Munster. Before opening Hear Clear in Maynooth in 2017, she worked in the audiology department at Temple Street Children’s Hospital and later in private practice in Galway.

She is a full member of the Irish Society of Hearing Aid Audiologists (MISHAA) and completes a minimum of thirty hours of continuing education every year, usually split between the British Society of Audiology conference and manufacturer-led paediatric training. She particularly enjoys fitting first-time hearing-aid patients and working with children on the autism spectrum.

Maeve lives in Kilcock with her husband and two daughters. We come to you

Home visits & accessibility.

Not everyone can easily travel to a clinic. We try to remove that barrier wherever possible — by visiting you at home, or by making sure the clinic itself is comfortable for every patient.

Home hearing assessments

Suitable for housebound patients, recent stroke or hip replacement recovery, or anyone for whom a clinic visit is impractical. Full portable audiometry equipment, same quality of assessment as in-clinic.

We cover all of Kildare, Meath, North Dublin and West Wicklow for home visits. Bookings usually available within 7 days.

€150 + €0.50/km mileage from Maynooth

Wheelchair access at the clinic

Level access at the Main Street entrance, no steps inside. Accessible WC on the ground floor. Our consult room doorway is 92 cm wide and accommodates most standard and powered wheelchairs.

Parking: two designated accessible spaces directly outside the clinic on Main Street (free with a disabled parking badge).

Hearing-loop-compatible consult rooms

Both consult rooms have a T-coil induction loop fitted. If you already wear a hearing aid with a T-setting, you can use it during the consultation instead of relying on the room acoustics.

We also offer live captions on a tablet for patients who find speech-reading easier than relying on residual hearing.
