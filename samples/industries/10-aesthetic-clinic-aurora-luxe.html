What we treat

Four areas of clinical practice.

01 / 04

Fine lines and dynamic wrinkles

We treat forehead lines, frown lines between the brows, and crow's feet using botulinum toxin type A (Bocouture, Azzalure, Botox — all authorised in Ireland). Dosing is individualised at the consultation stage and reviewed at fourteen days. Where a patient is unlikely to benefit, we will say so.

Typical course: single session, reviewed at 14 days Duration of effect: 3–4 months Downtime: minimal
02 / 04

Volume loss and facial balance

Hyaluronic acid dermal fillers (Juvederm, Restylane, Belotero) are used to address age-related volume loss in the mid-face, temples, and jawline. We work conservatively: small volumes, staged sessions, and a written plan. We do not treat lips on a first visit and we do not offer same-day treatment.

Products: HA fillers, CE-marked, EU-sourced Sessions: typically 1–3, spaced 2–4 weeks Reversible: yes, with hyaluronidase
03 / 04

Skin quality and texture

For patients concerned with skin texture, pore appearance, and early photoageing, we offer Profhilo bio-remodelling, medical-grade chemical peels (lactic, mandelic, and TCA at appropriate depths), and Sunekos for the peri-orbital area. Treatment selection follows a VISIA skin assessment at the consultation.

Assessment: VISIA imaging Courses: usually 2–3 sessions Aftercare: included in fee
04 / 04

Hair and beard thinning

Polynucleotide-based protocols (Nucleofill Hair, Plinest Hair) and exosome therapies are used in cases of androgenetic thinning and early-stage alopecia, subject to a medical history review. We will refer patients with autoimmune hair loss, rapid-onset alopecia, or systemic triggers to a consultant dermatologist where appropriate.

Assessment required: medical history, trichoscopy Courses: 3–4 sessions over 3 months Referral pathway: when clinically indicated

Our practice standards

How this clinic is run.

  1. S01

    No before/after photography

    We do not use before and after photographs in our marketing. We consider the practice inconsistent with the Irish Medical Council's guidance on advertising medical services and with patient dignity. If you wish to see images of a specific treatment for clinical understanding, they will be shown to you at consultation, in private, with identifying features removed.

  2. S02

    No treatment on a first visit

    The first appointment is always a consultation. We will not treat you on the same day. A cooling-off interval of at least 72 hours exists between the consultation and any treatment, and you are free to decline at any point without charge beyond the consultation fee.

  3. S03

    Authorised products, authorised sources

    All prescription-only medicines and medical devices used in the clinic are sourced from authorised EU suppliers, stored to manufacturer specification, and tracked by batch and expiry on the patient record. We do not use unlicensed or grey-market products under any circumstance.

  4. S04

    Adverse event reporting

    Any suspected adverse reaction to a medicinal product is reported to the Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA) via the yellow card scheme. Our practitioners are trained in recognising vascular occlusion, hypersensitivity reactions, and delayed nodules, and we hold hyaluronidase on-site for reversal of hyaluronic acid fillers.

  5. S05

    Complaints procedure

    If you are dissatisfied with any aspect of your care, you may raise it with us directly in writing and will receive a written response within fourteen days. You may additionally refer the matter to the Medical Council of Ireland, whose complaints procedure is published at medicalcouncil.ie. Both pathways are independent of clinic management.

  6. S06

    Pregnancy, breastfeeding, and minors

    We do not perform injectable treatments on patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding. We do not treat patients under the age of eighteen under any circumstance, nor between 18 and 21 without a documented clinical indication reviewed by the Medical Director.

  7. S07

    Medical records

    Patient records are held electronically on an Irish-hosted clinical system, encrypted at rest and retained for eight years from last contact per Medical Council guidance. Access is limited to the treating clinician and the practice manager. A copy of your record can be requested in writing under the General Data Protection Regulation.

A first visit

Six steps, over two appointments.

Step 01

Book a consultation

By phone or email, Tuesday or Friday. The €80 fee is taken at booking and credited against any treatment you choose to proceed with.

45 minutes · daytime
Step 02

Medical history & skin assessment

A written medical history is taken, followed by a clinical examination and VISIA skin imaging. Current medications, allergies, and prior treatments are recorded on your file.

Included in consultation
Step 03

A treatment plan, written

You receive a written plan with the proposed treatment, products, dosing, expected outcome, and full fee. You are not asked to book anything in the room.

Emailed within 24 hours
Step 04

Cooling-off period

A minimum of 72 hours between consultation and treatment. You may decline, defer, or request changes. If you choose not to proceed, the consultation fee is not refunded but the assessment is yours.

At least 3 days
Step 05

Treatment day

Treatment is performed by the consulting doctor. You may bring a chaperone. Written consent is taken again, and you receive aftercare instructions and an emergency contact on departure.

30–60 minutes
Step 06

Review at fourteen days

A follow-up appointment is included in the fee for all injectable treatments. Photographs are taken for the clinical record only and are not used externally.

15 minutes
