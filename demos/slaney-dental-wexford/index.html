Your dentist

Dr. Ciarán O’Sullivan.

Ciarán has practised dentistry in Wexford Town for over two decades. He trained at the National University of Ireland and completed his post-graduate Membership in the Faculty of Dentistry at the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland. He believes good dental care is plain, steady and gentle — not rushed, never pushy, and never over-sold.

Most of Slaney Dental’s patients are local Wexford families who have been coming for years, often across three generations. New patients are welcome any time.
