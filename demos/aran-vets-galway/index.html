What We Do

Compassionate care, six days a week

From first vaccinations to complex surgery, our team of four qualified vets and nurses provides thorough, unhurried care for all small animals. Same-day emergency appointments available.

  1. 01

    Small Animal Consultations

    Thorough health assessments for dogs, cats, rabbits, and small mammals. Same-day slots Monday to Saturday.

  2. 02

    Emergency & Urgent Care

    Same-day emergency appointments during hours. Out-of-hours emergency line available for existing clients.

  3. 03

    Vaccinations & Preventative Care

    Core and non-core vaccines, parasite control, nutrition planning, and annual wellness checks.

  4. 04

    Dental & Oral Health

    Dental assessments, scale and polish, extractions under safe anaesthetic. In-house dental x-ray.

  5. 05

    Spay & Neuter

    Day-procedure spay and neuter surgery with full pre-op and post-op care. Modern anaesthetic monitoring.

  6. 06

    Microchipping & Passports

    Quick microchipping appointments and EU pet passport issuing. We handle all paperwork for you.
