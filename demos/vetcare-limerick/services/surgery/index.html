Surgical Services

Soft-tissue and orthopaedic surgery on-site. Full anaesthetic monitoring, in-house lab, and digital x-ray.

About this service

Our on-site surgical suite is equipped to handle the full range of soft-tissue and orthopaedic procedures without the stress of referral to an unfamiliar practice. Led by Aoife O'Brien MVB MRCVS GPCert(SAS), our surgical team combines technical precision with genuine compassion for anxious owners.

Every surgical patient receives a thorough pre-operative assessment, including blood screening and chest x-ray where appropriate. We use modern, evidence-based anaesthetic protocols and our theatre is equipped with multi-parameter monitors, a fluid pump, and a heated operating table. Digital x-ray and an in-house laboratory mean we have all the diagnostics we need on the day.

What to expect

  • ✓ Pre-operative assessment including blood panel and imaging as required
  • ✓ Dedicated theatre nurse monitoring anaesthesia throughout the procedure
  • ✓ Digital x-ray and in-house laboratory results available same day
  • ✓ Post-operative pain management plan and scheduled follow-up appointment
