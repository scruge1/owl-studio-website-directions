Dental Care

Dental assessments, scale and polish, extractions under safe anaesthetic.

About this service

Dental disease is one of the most common — and most under-treated — conditions in dogs and cats. Studies suggest that over 80% of pets over the age of three have some degree of periodontal disease. Left untreated, dental pain affects quality of life and can lead to serious systemic health problems.

Our dedicated dental theatre is equipped with an ultrasonic scaler, dental x-ray, and the full range of instruments for extractions and oral surgery. All dental procedures are carried out under general anaesthesia so we can work thoroughly and safely. We'll send you home with a clear post-procedure care plan and guidance on at-home dental maintenance.

What to expect

  • ✓ Dental assessment under anaesthesia — we cannot fully examine a conscious patient's mouth
  • ✓ Ultrasonic scale and polish to remove tartar above and below the gum line
  • ✓ Dental x-ray to assess tooth roots and bone before any extraction decision
  • ✓ Post-procedure care plan and home dental care recommendations
