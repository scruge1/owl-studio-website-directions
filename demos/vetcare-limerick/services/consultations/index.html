Small Animal Consultations

Thorough health assessments for dogs, cats, and small mammals. Same-day slots available Monday–Saturday.

About this service

Every consultation at Limerick Vet Clinic begins with a thorough, unhurried examination. Whether you're bringing in a new puppy for their first health check or a senior cat with a long-standing condition, our vets take the time to understand your pet's history and your concerns before reaching any conclusions. We believe that a well-informed owner is as important as the clinical examination itself.

Our consulting rooms are designed to be calm and spacious — we know that a vet visit can be stressful for animals and owners alike. Same-day appointments are available Monday through Saturday, and our reception team will always advise you on the most appropriate vet to see based on your pet's needs.

What to expect

  • ✓ A full nose-to-tail physical examination by one of our RCVS-registered vets
  • ✓ Clear explanation of any findings and recommended next steps
  • ✓ Same-day lab results available through our in-house laboratory if required
  • ✓ Printed or emailed summary of the consultation for your records
