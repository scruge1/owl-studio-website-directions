Neutering & Microchipping

Day-procedure neutering with pre-op and post-op care. Microchipping while you wait.

About this service

Neutering is one of the most important decisions you'll make for your pet's long-term health. At Limerick Vet Clinic, all neutering procedures are carried out as day procedures under full general anaesthesia, with continuous monitoring by our nursing team throughout. Our vets will advise you on the ideal age and timing for your specific pet — this varies by species, breed, and individual development.

We also offer microchipping as a standalone appointment — a quick, low-stress procedure that provides permanent identification and is a legal requirement for all dogs in Ireland. We register your pet's chip details on the national database on the same day.

What to expect

  • ✓ Pre-operative blood screening available for older or high-risk patients
  • ✓ Full anaesthetic monitoring with dedicated nurse throughout the procedure
  • ✓ Post-operative care instructions and a follow-up check included
  • ✓ Microchipping available as a standalone walk-in or combined with any appointment
