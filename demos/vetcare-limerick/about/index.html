Our Story

Limerick Vet Clinic was founded in 1998 by Siobhán Murphy after years in referral practice across Ireland. What started as a small-animal clinic has grown into a six-vet practice serving thousands of families across Limerick, Clare, and Tipperary.

From the very beginning, our founding principle has been simple: treat every patient as if they were your own. That philosophy has built us a loyal community of pet owners who trust us not just for routine care, but for the moments that matter most — the emergencies, the surgeries, the difficult decisions.

Today we operate a purpose-built clinic on Lower Gerald Griffin Street, equipped with an on-site surgical suite, digital x-ray, in-house laboratory, and dedicated dental theatre. We're open six days a week, with an out-of-hours emergency line available to all existing clients.

RCVS Accredited Practice

The Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons Practice Standards Scheme accredits veterinary practices that demonstrate exceptional standards of care, facilities, and professional development. Accreditation is not automatic — it requires an inspection and ongoing commitment to meeting the RCVS's strict criteria.

Limerick Vet Clinic has held RCVS Practice Accreditation continuously since 2008. It means you can trust that every aspect of your pet's care — from the cleanliness of our consulting rooms to the qualifications of our nursing team — meets the highest professional standards in veterinary practice.
