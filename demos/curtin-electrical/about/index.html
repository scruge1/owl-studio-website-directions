PC Padraig 'Paddy' Curtin

Meet Paddy

Owner & Master Electrician

Paddy founded Curtin Electrical in 2003 after years working with major contractors across Munster. Born and raised in Limerick, he built the business on a simple rule: show up on time, do the job right, charge a fair price.

23 years later, that rule hasn't changed. Paddy handles every job personally — you won't deal with a subcontractor or a call centre. When you ring, you get Paddy.
