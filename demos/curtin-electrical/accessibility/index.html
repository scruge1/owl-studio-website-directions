Accessibility Statement

Last updated: April 2026

Our commitment

Curtin Electrical Services is committed to making this website accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Measures taken

  • All text meets a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its background
  • All interactive elements have a minimum touch target size of 44×44px on mobile
  • A visible skip-to-main-content link is provided at the top of every page
  • All images have descriptive alt text
  • The site uses semantic HTML5 landmarks (header, main, footer, nav)
  • All animations respect the prefers-reduced-motion media query
  • Body text is set at a minimum of 17px on mobile devices
  • All phone numbers are presented as tap-to-call links

Known limitations

This site is a prototype/demonstration built for Owl Studio. As such, some third-party content linked from this site (such as Google Maps or social media embeds) may not fully meet accessibility requirements.
