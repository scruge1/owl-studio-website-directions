Our Story
The Atmosphere

Where Cork gathers

Since 1987, Clancy's on Grand Parade has been the address Cork returns to for evenings that matter. The candlelight, the quiet hum of conversation, the care in every plate.

The Menu

Sourced from Munster's finest

Our à la carte menu changes with what's best from Cork's farmers and fishermen. Aged Hereford from Macroom. Atlantic cod from Castletownbere. Each dish a dedication to the county.
