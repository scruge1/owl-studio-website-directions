# Lead log — append-only

One line per call. Format:

```
YYYY-MM-DD · <business> · <location> · <style picked> · <tier asked> · booked/audit/no/follow-up · <brief note>
```

Use the note field for what closed or didn't close — the whole
pitch-refinement loop depends on patterns here after 10–20 calls.
